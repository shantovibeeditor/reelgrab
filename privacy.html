Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

  • ReelGrab runs on your Mac. There's no ReelGrab account and no ReelGrab server that sees your videos.
  • Your clips, settings and license stay on your Mac.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, in the app or on this website.
  • The app talks to a few outside services only to do its job (YouTube, tool downloads, license checks). They're all listed below.

This policy covers the ReelGrab Mac app and this website. ReelGrab is made and run in Bangladesh ("we"). Questions? Message us on WhatsApp.

1. What stays on your Mac

ReelGrab stores these only on your computer, in ~/Library/Application Support/ReelGrab and the folder where you save clips:

  • Your clips: the files you download.
  • Settings: your save folder, your caption and format choices, and your Anthropic API key if you add one for AI boost.
  • Your license: your license key, and the name or email attached to it.
  • Daily usage counts on the free plan (how many clips and analyses today).
  • A log file with technical details (which videos and time ranges were processed, errors) to help fix problems. It never leaves your Mac unless you send it to us.

We can't see any of this. Uninstalling ReelGrab and deleting that folder removes it.

2. Outside services the app uses

bKash / Nagad license keys are checked entirely on your Mac and need no internet.

3. When you buy or contact us

  • bKash / Nagad: you send us your payment on WhatsApp. We keep your name, phone number, transaction ID and the license key we gave you, to support you and to stop key sharing.
  • Card (Gumroad): Gumroad handles the payment and tax, and shares your name and email with us as the seller. We never see your card details. See Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • Feedback and support: the app's Feedback button opens WhatsApp with your ReelGrab version, macOS version, Mac type (Apple Silicon or Intel), your plan, and the video link if one is loaded. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

We use this information only to deliver your license, help you and improve ReelGrab. We don't sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

4. This website

The site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads. Our web host may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security.

5. How long we keep things

Purchase and license records are kept for as long as your license is active and as required for tax and accounting. Support chats stay in WhatsApp until deleted. You can ask us to delete your details at any time; we'll keep only what we're legally required to.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it: message us on WhatsApp.
  • Remove your license from a Mac in ReelGrab (★ Premium → Remove from this Mac).
  • Delete everything ReelGrab stored locally by removing ~/Library/Application Support/ReelGrab.

7. Children

ReelGrab is meant for adults and working creators. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, mention it in the app or on this page.
