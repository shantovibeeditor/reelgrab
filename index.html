FAQ

Questions, answered.

What videos can I clip?

YouTube, TikTok and public Facebook links, and video files on your Mac (MP4, MOV, MKV and more). Use videos you have the right to use: your own channel, your clients' videos, Creative Commons videos, or videos you have permission to repost. ReelGrab doesn't remove DRM or bypass paywalls.

Does it download the whole video?

No. Only the parts you mark are downloaded, cut frame-accurately. A 30-second clip from a 2-hour podcast takes seconds.

Is it Mac only?

Yes, for now: macOS 11 or later, on Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) and Intel Macs. A Windows version is planned.

I paid with bKash. How do I get my key?

Send the transaction ID on WhatsApp. You'll get your license key there. Paste it into ReelGrab (Upgrade, then Activate) and Premium turns on.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free gives you 3 clips and 1 viral analysis a day at up to 1080p, with the very best viral moments locked. Premium removes every limit, adds 4K, and is a one-time payment for 2 Macs.

What do I need on first launch?

An internet connection. ReelGrab downloads its video tools once (about 330 MB, a couple of minutes), then it's ready.

Can it make Bangla captions for a video that has none?

Yes. ReelGrab listens to the Bangla speech and writes the captions on your Mac. The speech model is a one-time 539 MB download (one click in the app). After that it works offline, and a 1-minute clip takes about 15 seconds.

macOS says ReelGrab "can't be opened". What do I do?

Click Done, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to ReelGrab. You only do this once. The same steps are in the "First time - open me" file inside the download.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If ReelGrab doesn't work on your Mac, message us within 7 days of buying for a full refund.
