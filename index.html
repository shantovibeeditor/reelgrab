Does it work on Windows?

Yes, in beta: Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Download ReelBhai-Setup.exe and double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway (you only see this once). If YouTube asks "are you a bot?", install Firefox, sign in to YouTube there and try again. The same Premium key works on Mac and Windows.

How do I get my key after paying?

Send your bKash or Nagad transaction ID on WhatsApp. We send the key back. In ReelBhai, click Upgrade, then Activate, and paste it.

macOS says it "can't be opened". What now?

Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. If it says "damaged", open Terminal, paste xattr -cr /Applications/ReelBhai.app and press Enter.

What videos can I use?
